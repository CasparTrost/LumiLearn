# Pictures of "Reise mit Lumi" (public/sprites/journey/)

colour black outline, medium shading) in November 2026, when the CometAPI
image quota was used up. The prompts, to make a picture again in the same style:

| chest.png | 64 x 64 | closed wooden treasure chest with golden metal corners and a golden lock, cute game item |
| chest-open.png | 64 x 64, palette of chest.png | open wooden treasure chest with golden corners, the lid open, bright golden light and sparkles shining out of it, cute game item |

| flag.png | 64 x 64 | small wooden signpost with a red triangle pennant flag on top, standing on a little tuft of green grass, game icon |
